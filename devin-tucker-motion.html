About

I film live events and make short films about movement. I'm also a professional circus artist, juggler, and teacher in Portland, so I'm comfortable around performers and in rooms where things move fast.

Most of my event work is for people who want a film they will actually rewatch and share. The art films are collaborations, and I'm always glad to hear a new idea.
