Video editing in Portland, Oregon

Bring me footage you have shot and I will shape it into a finished film, a teaser, or a reel. I edit events, performances, and promotional videos, and I care about rhythm, sound, and what to leave out.

Send me your projectBook a short call

What I edit

  • Event films. Raw coverage shaped into a highlight film or a set of longer cuts.
  • Performance and dance films. Work made for the screen, or a live show cut so it is worth watching.
  • Teasers and trailers. Short, sharp pieces to announce a show or an event.
  • Showreels. A reel for a performer, a company, or a videographer.

How it works

Send the footage. A link to a shared folder is enough. Tell me what the film is for, how long it should run, and where it will be seen.

We agree the rounds. Before I start, we settle how many rounds of feedback are included, so there are no surprises.

I send a first cut. You tell me what works and what does not, and I refine it.

Then I finish it. Sound, color, titles, and the files you need for every place the film will live.

Music and rights

Music has to be licensed for the way the film will be used. If you already have music, tell me where it comes from. If you do not, I can work with licensed tracks.

Remote or in person

Most editing happens remotely, with footage arriving by link. If you are in Portland and would rather review a cut together, we can meet.

Questions

What footage can you work with?

Most professional and consumer cameras. Tell me what you shot on and roughly how much there is.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length and complexity of the film. Tell me your deadline when you write, and I will say plainly whether it works.

What does it cost?

I quote by the project, once I know how much footage there is and what you want to end up with.
