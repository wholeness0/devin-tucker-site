Event videography in Portland, Oregon

I film galas, conferences, concerts, and celebrations around Portland, then edit the day into a film you can share. The aim is a film people want to watch twice: the speeches that mattered, the room, and the moments nobody scheduled.

What I film

  • Galas and fundraisers. The room, the speeches, and the people who make the night work.
  • Conferences and talks. Speakers, panels, and the conversations in the hallway.
  • Concerts and live performances. The stage, the crowd, and the moments between songs.
  • Celebrations. Parties, openings, and anniversaries worth keeping.

How it works

First, we talk. Tell me the date, the place, and what the film is for. We decide what has to be captured and what is a bonus.

Then I plan the coverage. I ask about the schedule, any speeches or performances, and the venue, and I arrive early to set up.

On the day, I stay out of the way. I film from the edges, avoid interrupting, and work alongside your staff and any photographers.

Afterward, I edit. You get a highlight film you can share, and longer cuts of speeches or performances if you ask for them.

What you can ask for

A highlight film for sharing. Full-length cuts of speeches, talks, or performances. Short versions for social media. If you need something else, ask.

Filming in Portland

Filming indoors at a private venue is usually settled between you, the venue, and me. Filming on streets, sidewalks, or in city parks is different. The City of Portland's Events and Film Office is the place to start for permits, and street and sidewalk permits ask for at least three business days' notice and proof of insurance. Many venues also ask vendors for a certificate of insurance. If your plans involve any of this, tell me early and we will sort it out together.

Questions

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Dates fill, especially in event season, so write as soon as you have one.

What does it cost?

It depends on how long the event runs, how many places I need to be, and what you want back. Tell me about your day and I will send a quote.

Who owns the film?

We agree this in writing before the event: what you can use the film for, and what I can show in my own portfolio.

Do you work outside Portland?

Mostly in and around Portland. If your event is further away, ask.
