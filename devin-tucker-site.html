More about me

I film events and make short films about movement, and I edit everything myself.

I film events because the best moments are never on the schedule. I film circus and dance because I perform and teach it, so I know when to move closer and when to stay out of the way.

I often work with Slipstream Circus Collective, the Portland circus and digital arts collective I founded.
